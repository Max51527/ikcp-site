## Design Specificity Verdict

**Specific in its symbols, generic in its structure — and the specific parts are the ones that do not render.**

Strip the tricolour balloon, the "Prendre de la hauteur" pun the balloon literally illustrates, the CGI source citations, and the heading "Deux choses, et rien d'autre.", and what remains is the standard French wealthtech template: hero-left copy + hero-right dark card, stat bar, trust-badge row, multi-step calculator with sticky dark score panel, founder bio + four value cards, four-column footer.

Worse: `.sky-block` — the flying tricolour balloon, the "Marcel · En ligne" badge, "Essayer Marcel gratuitement" and "Voir IKCP en 90 s" — computes to `display:none` at 1280px AND at 375px. The most brand-specific asset on the page ships as ~90 lines of dead SVG. What renders instead is an abstract two-circle orbit diagram that could belong to any AI product.

Note: dark-glow at line 809 was already reduced today; the remaining instance is inside a keyframe.

## Overall Impression

The page argues: promise → tool → 470 words of justified prose about a founder and a practice that is closed to new clients → what we actually sell. The four deletions made today did not leave a hole between hero and offer; they left **"Le Cabinet"** as the load-bearing bridge — a non-sequitur wedged between the two things a visitor came for, selling the one thing that is not for sale.

The single biggest opportunity: move "Deux choses, et rien d'autre." to immediately after the hero. It is the best writing on the site and it currently arrives at 75% depth, after the reader has decided.

## What's Working

1. **The sources block under the simulator.** Citing economie.gouv.fr and three CGI articles turns the calculator from a flattery machine into an auditable instrument. It invites verification — the opposite of what a lead-capture calculator normally does. The most credible object on the page.
2. **The opt-in's consent architecture.** Consent first, fields second (fields are x-shown only after consent), submit gated. This inverts the industry default, so the decision precedes the disclosure. Reads as respect, not compliance theatre.
3. **"Deux choses, et rien d'autre."** The asymmetric card pair encodes the product split (cream = the artefact, dark = the intelligence), and "C'est le travail qu'une intelligence artificielle généraliste ne peut pas faire" names the competitor without naming it — stating the moat as a limitation of the alternative rather than a boast. "Il ne part pas d'une page blanche : il part de vous." is the best sentence on the site.

## Priority Issues

### [P0] On mobile, the only navigation control is physically unclickable
`document.elementFromPoint()` at the centre of the ☰ button returns `SPAN.music-status`. The music player (`#music-player`, z-index 200) sits on top of `<nav>` (z-index 50). Tapping the menu opens the ambient-music panel. Below 1024px the nav CTA is `hidden lg:flex`, so the mobile bar offers zero navigation and zero CTA — 190px of pure chrome.
**Why it matters**: mobile visitors cannot reach /marcel, /cabinet or the account CTA from the nav at all. An ambient music toggle is beating the primary navigation of a wealth-management site.
**Fix**: move `#music-player` to z-index 40 and offset it right:76px below 1024px (or hide under lg). Add the CTA to the mobile bar. Drop the two-line logo lockup to one line.
**Suggested command**: /impeccable adapt

### [P0] The offer sits at 75% page depth, behind a bio for a closed practice
`#bilan-patrimonial` ends at 3 105px; `#philosophie` runs 3 123→3 935; the offer sits at 3 935 of 5 222 (8 325 on mobile). All seven CTAs above it ask for a decision the page has not yet justified.
**Why it matters**: the product is two things and the cabinet is closed, yet the closed cabinet gets 812px of prime real estate between the tool and the offer.
**Fix**: move "Deux choses, et rien d'autre." to immediately after the hero. Cut `#philosophie` to a three-line credibility strip folded into the offer, and delete the four value cards — "meilleures solutions du marché" and "chaque recommandation" cannot survive a truthfulness pass anyway.
**Suggested command**: /impeccable distill

### [P1] Eight CTAs, seven labels, one destination — one of them lies
Eight elements point at /creer-mon-compte under seven different labels. "Écrire à Maxime →", sitting under "Vous préférez écrire directement à Maxime ?", promises human contact and silently delivers a signup form — a dark pattern and a product-truth violation in one link.
**Fix**: standardise on two strings — "Commencer mon bilan — gratuit" (primary) and "Créer mon compte" (nav). Delete the "Écrire à Maxime" block; it serves a channel that is closed.
**Suggested command**: /impeccable clarify

### [P1] Visible copy breaches the regulatory and truthfulness frame
Rendered live: "Votre potentiel d'économie annuelle : 3 840 €"; locked labels "Stratégie de capitalisation sur-mesure" and "Plan de donation et assurance-vie clause bénéficiaire"; "Le cabinet IKCP reviendra vers vous"; "Votre intérêt guide chaque recommandation"; "Les meilleures solutions du marché, sans compromis"; a footer phone number plus "Sur rendez-vous uniquement".
**Why it matters**: quantified promised results and personalised-recommendation language on a page whose own footer disclaims both (L.541-1), plus an unsolicited-contact promise and a rendez-vous channel the product says does not exist. "Meilleures solutions du marché" is unsubstantiable under L.121-2. No fabricated testimonials or usage numbers were found — that part is clean.
**Fix**: reframe every piste teaser as a question; replace locked labels with "Fiche + simulateur dans votre espace"; replace the confirmation with "C'est noté — vos pistes vous attendent dans votre espace"; delete "recommandation" and "meilleures solutions du marché"; drop the phone line and "sur rendez-vous uniquement".
**Suggested command**: /impeccable clarify

### [P2] Chrome and reveal animation eat the page
190px nav + 101px PWA banner (z-index 9999, pinned at y:692 of 812) + music pill leave roughly 500px of usable mobile viewport. Section headings are clipped by the fixed nav. `reveal.js` holds sections at opacity:0 — fast scroll yields blank viewports. 68 of 81 focusable elements have no visible focus indicator; 46 of 83 tap targets are under 44×44 on mobile; `/icons/maxime.jpg` returns 404.
**Fix**: `scroll-margin-top: 200px` on section anchors; auto-dismiss the PWA banner after one viewport and persist it; `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce) { [reveal] { opacity: 1 !important } }` plus a failsafe timeout; add a visible focus ring; fix or remove the missing image.
**Suggested command**: /impeccable audit
